# Grouping rules

These rules tell Claude how to sort and describe saved posts. They are sent word for word at the start of every grouping request, so write them as plain instructions. Edit freely, or ask your agent to ("put cooking videos in Recipes").

New rules apply to posts grouped from then on. Run `saved-digest regroup` to re-sort the posts already stored.

## Groups

Put each post in the first group that fits:

- AI and coding: Claude/Codex skills, plugins, MCP servers, repos, tools, prompts and dev tips: "Dev & AI tips".
- UI, web, graphic and product design, typography, animation: "Design".
- Films, TV shows and clips from them: "Films & TV".
- Books and reading recommendations: "Books".
- Songs, albums, artists and music production: "Music".
- Recipes and cooking: "Recipes".
- Workouts, training, health and sleep: "Fitness & health".
- Restaurants, shops and travel spots: "Places to go".
- Maths, science, history and other things explained: "Learning".
- Anything else worth keeping: a short group name of 1-3 words.
- Memes, ads and spam with nothing to learn or revisit: "Ignore" (hidden from All in the web app).

## Summaries

- Dev & AI tips: what the tool or technique does and when you would use it.
- Films & TV: the title and year, and whether it is a film or a series.
- Books: the title and author.
- Recipes: the dish and its main ingredients.
- Places to go: the name and the city.
